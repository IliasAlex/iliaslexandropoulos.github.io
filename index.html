About

Research at the intersection of signals, perception, and hardware.

I am a PhD student at Southern Illinois University Carbondale, advised by Prof. Spyros Tragoudas. My doctoral research focuses on perception-aware and hardware-efficient signal processing.

My work brings together audio and image processing, artificial intelligence, energy-aware computing, and acceleration on NPUs and FPGAs. I am interested in systems that direct computation and preservation toward the parts of a signal that matter most to people.

Research

Selected directions

My research asks how intelligent systems can protect what people perceive while using less energy and computation.

01

Audio · Speech Enhancement

Perception-aware speech enhancement

Learning systems that reduce noise without removing short, perceptually important components of a target speaker.

↗
02

Vision · Energy-Aware Displays

Energy-aware image processing

Optimizing luminance and contrast for OLED power reduction while preserving image structure and perceptual quality.

↗
03

Hardware · Efficient AI

NPU and FPGA acceleration

Mapping perceptual signal-processing models onto specialized hardware under latency, power, and resource constraints.

↗
04

AI · Next-Generation Networks

Intelligent network systems

Earlier work on intent-driven 6G systems, adversarial robustness, and graph-based indoor localization.
